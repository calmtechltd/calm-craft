## Representative composition and recovery

An assigned verified lead is queued through `queueResearch`; its versioned run carries assignment, campaign definition, knowledge, ICP, authority and qualification policy bindings. Programme admission claims the exact research worker and starts the existing research Workflow. The action asks `researchSteps.context` for a classified next checkpoint, prepares the bounded command/attempt, then the gateway reserves and claims paid work before provider invocation. Crawl polling retains only the exact saved job; deterministic candidate handling and current qualification produce evidence sufficient for drafting or a held result.

On evaluator unavailability, saved candidates and the acquisition remain retained. Public `research.control` authorizes the actor/business, accepts an idempotent exact-run control, checks expected revision and resume/retry state, checks recovery and readiness, updates the worker and retained run, increments revision, schedules a command-bound programme wake and completes the receipt in one mutation. Repeated delivery returns the first receipt. Qualification recovery must not crawl again. Stop/pause prevent subsequent qualification and dispatch; an already submitted crawl may be polled for original result retention only. An uncertain effect blocks replacement work and remains tied to its original operation. See the contract for the distinction between this checkpoint path and a new research version.

## Boundary decisions and gaps

The shared journey already delegates research control to the research owner. Preserve that seam. The selected contract documents ownership around existing functions; it does not require a new scheduler, workflow runtime, repository adapter, package or classes.

`researchRecoveryBlocker`, `recoverResearch` and `researchReady` return nullable or ordinary strings; state selection and first-receipt reuse are currently distributed between callers and helper. A small typed recovery facade can make those obligations discoverable and harder to bypass, provided it preserves the public transaction. This is an evidence-backed interface improvement proposal, not proof of a product defect.

The recovery predicate inspects current and prior operation commands but checks receipt presence and selected spend states, while `researchContinuation.resolvedEffects` explicitly checks same-business command/subject, receipt or resolution uncertainty and settled/released/overrun spend. These paths govern different recovery operations; they must not be blindly unified. The selected adoption slice must prove the reachable effect invariants before treating the narrower predicate as sufficient. Source alone does not establish an unsafe reachable state.

Preparation and lead research use similar providers but have different policy: preparation is platform-funded, retains required children and may produce a zero-experiment review; assignment research binds campaign recipient eligibility, customer authority, paid accounting and qualified draft evidence. This is not evidence for a shared policy package. No second product was inspected; extraction is not recommended.

## Adoption slices

1. Reconcile this draft with accepted architecture and existing contract IDs. Exit: one selected capability entry, no duplicate governing authority, and an explicit owner for the public control transaction.
2. Define a discriminated recovery result at the existing local seam and migrate its three real caller classes. Keep `research.control`, registered internal entry paths, existing run/worker/command/effect identities and schema generation workflow stable. Exit: callers cannot bypass state/authority/effect predicates; maintenance retains its bounded service-owned recovery policy; diagnostics stay fixture-only; the revision/wake/receipt transaction remains atomic.
3. Verify meaningful recovery invariants using targeted fixture tests and add only demonstrated missing cases. Exit: exact-stage/no-reacquisition, scope/state/version blockers, original-job polling after stop, unknown-effect identity, attempt limit, duplicate receipts and atomic scheduling have passing evidence. The contract stays proposed until accepted; implementation/verification status must cite actual results.

No application changes, folder moves, extraction, provider calls or broad gates are part of this trial.
