The contracts should cite those authorities rather than duplicate them.

Inspected Astrophage's research workflow, checkpoint/action entry points, next-checkpoint selection, provider gateway, sourced-fact extraction and selected evidence ownership/fence operations. This is not a complete audit of either provider stack, tenancy model or all callers.
